# My job search profile

Copy to `config/profile.md` (gitignored) and write it in your own words. The Screener
reads this to decide, for jobs that already pass every hard rule, whether appa may
apply automatically or should queue the job for you.

It only ever sees the company name, job title and location. It never sees posting
text, so a posting can't argue its way into or out of your queue.

Hard rules (sponsorship, citizenship, clearance, years of experience) are not here.
They live in `config/profile.yaml` and are enforced by code.

## Apply myself (queue these for me)

- Big tech: FAANG+ and peers (Google, Meta, Apple, Amazon, Netflix, Microsoft, Nvidia,
  Salesforce, Adobe, Uber, Airbnb, ...).
- Companies that are big in software even if they aren't household names: Datadog,
  HubSpot, Stripe, Snowflake, Databricks, Cloudflare, MongoDB, Atlassian, Shopify, and
  similar established software companies.
- Well-known, well-funded startups where a strong, tailored application matters
  (for example top AI labs and fast-growing developer-tools companies).
- Roles that match my strongest experience unusually well (describe yours here).

## Fine to auto-apply

- Smaller or lesser-known companies, staffing-free direct postings, and early-stage
  startups that aren't on the list above.

## When unsure

Queue it for me.
