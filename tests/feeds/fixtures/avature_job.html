Description & Requirements

The Role

Bloomberg is looking for a Software Engineer to help build, modernize and operate the platforms that power our marketing websites and digital experiences. You will work on highly visible digital properties that connect Bloomberg's products, services and brand with audiences around the world.

This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who enjoys solving real business problems, taking ownership and delivering results. You'll work across our web ecosystem—from CMS platforms and frontend experiences to APIs, integrations, cloud infrastructure and developer tooling.

We're looking for engineers who are self-driven and comfortable taking ownership of problems that are not fully defined. You should be able to understand the desired outcome, ask the right questions, work with stakeholders and teammates to determine the right solution, and drive it through implementation and production.

You'll be part of a collaborative engineering team, so technical ability alone isn't enough. Strong communication, teamwork, curiosity and a willingness to help others succeed are essential.
